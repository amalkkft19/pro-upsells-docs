---
title: Read your analytics
description: Find the analytics for your offers, the Slide Cart and Web Push, and compare results before and after a change.
---

Each widget has an **Analytics** tab with results for its offers, and each offer has its own **Analytics** action. The app's home page shows an overview for the last 30 days, including **Revenue**, **Impressions** and **Active offers**. The Slide Cart and Web Push have their own analytics.

## How to use them

<Steps>

1. Pick one thing to change at a time, such as the discount or the product.
2. Note the date and leave the change long enough to collect orders.
3. Compare results before and after over a similar period, then keep whichever did better.

</Steps>
