title: Add a widget block to your theme
description: Embedded offers need a block in your theme to decide where the widget appears. Add one from the app or the theme editor.
---

Embedded offers need a block, which decides where the widget appears.

## The quick way

When you choose **Embedded** in an offer, the app shows **Click here to add the block**. Click it, position the block, then click **Save**.

<Screenshot
  src="add-block-banner.png"
  alt="The banner in the offer form that asks you to add the widget block to your theme, with a link to open the theme editor."
  caption="The banner in the offer form that opens the theme editor with the block ready."
/>

## From the theme editor

<Steps>

1. Go to **Online Store > Themes** and click **Customize**.
2. Choose the page type at the top of the editor, such as **Products**.
3. Click **Add block** in the sidebar, under the section you want.
4. Open the **Apps** tab.
5. Choose the block.
6. Drag it into position.
7. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  src="theme-editor-block.png"

## Block names

| Widget | Block name |
|---|---|
| Upsell & Cross-sell | **Upsell & Cross-sell** |
| Frequently Bought Together | **FBT Upsell** |
| Volume Discount | **Volume Discount** |
| AI Recommendations | **AI Recommendation** |

<Callout type="note">
Add only one block of each type to a page. An offer shows once, in the first block. A second block stays empty, and in the theme editor it shows a note instead of the offer.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
An empty block shows nothing to shoppers. In the theme editor, it shows a note until a matching offer is active.
</Callout>
