---
title: Add a block to checkout, thank you or order status
description: Offers on the checkout, thank you and order status pages use a checkout block. Add it in the Shopify checkout editor.
---

Offers on these pages use a checkout block rather than a theme block.

<Callout type="plus">
The **Checkout** page needs Shopify Plus. **Thank you** and **Order status** blocks work on all plans.
</Callout>

<Steps>

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Settings > Checkout**.
2. Click **Customize** on your live checkout, under **Configurations**.
3. Open the page menu at the top of the editor and select the page.
4. Click **Add block**.
5. Select **Upsell & Cross-sell** or **Volume Discount**.
6. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  src="checkout-editor-block.png"
  alt="A Pro Upsells block added to a page in the Shopify checkout editor."
  caption="A block in the checkout editor."
/>

Create the offer with the same **Display page** first, or the block stays empty.
