## General

- **Trigger condition:** what makes the offer show. Choose **Show for all products**, **Show for specific products**, **Show for specific collections** or **Show on above order / cart value**.
- **Display page:** where it appears.
- **Display type:** how it appears.
  - **Popup** opens when the shopper clicks **Add to Cart**, or **Checkout** on the cart page.
  - **Embedded** sits inside the page and needs a block on your theme.
  - **Popup on page load** opens as the page loads.
- **Offer type:** **Upsell**, **Cross-sell** or **None**.
- **Discounts:** the products, **Discount type** (**Percentage**, **Fixed amount** or **None**), **Discount value** and **Minimum purchase quantity**.

## Settings

- **Show quantity picker** and **Hide variant selector**.
- **Hide product if already in cart** and **Hide out-of-stock products**.
- **Primary button** and **Secondary button:** show or hide them and choose an **Action**: **Checkout**, **Go to cart** or **Close**.
- **Custom CSS:** for developers.
- **Placement:** where a widget goes when no block is on the page. A block always takes priority.

## Translations

Change the title and button words, for example into your store's language.

## Design

Choose a **Template theme** and set colours. The preview shows changes as you make them.

## Offer list actions

Each offer list also has **Duplicate**, **Delete**, **Analytics** and **Send Notification** actions.
