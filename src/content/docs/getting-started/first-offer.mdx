## Create the offer

<Steps>

1. Go to **Upsells** in the app and open **Upsells & Cross-sell**.
2. Click **Create**.
3. Enter a **Name** that only you will see.
4. Keep **Show for all products** under **When should the upsell be shown?**.
5. Choose **Product page** under **Display page**.
6. Choose **Embedded** under **Display type**.
7. Choose **Cross-sell** under **Offer type**.
8. Choose the product to recommend under **Discounts**.
9. Set the **Discount type** and **Discount value**, or choose **None**.
10. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

## Add the block

An embedded widget needs a block on your theme, so add one next. When **Embedded** is selected, the app shows a link, **Click here to add the block**, that opens the theme editor with the block ready. Click it, then click **Save** in the theme editor. See [Add a widget block to your theme](/guides/add-block-to-theme/).

<Callout type="tip">
Prefer no theme changes? Choose **Popup** under **Display type**. Popups don't need a block.
</Callout>
