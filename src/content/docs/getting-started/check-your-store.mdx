---
title: Check it on your store
description: Test your first Pro Upsells offer on your store in a private window, from the product page to checkout.
---

Test your offer the way a shopper would. You don't need to place an order.

<Steps>

1. Open a product page on your store in a private browser window.
2. Look for the offer where you placed the block.
3. Click **Add to cart** on the offer and check the product and discount in your cart.
4. Go to checkout and confirm the discount applies. You don't need to place the order.

</Steps>

## Back in the app

The **App status** section on the home page shows the block as active once it's placed.

If you don't see your offer, go to [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/).
