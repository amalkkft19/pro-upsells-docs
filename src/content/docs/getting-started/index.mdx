Pro Upsells adds offers to your Shopify store that encourage shoppers to buy more. It has three parts, all found in the app's menu:

- **Upsells:** offers shown on your store, such as recommendations, volume discounts and free gifts.
- **Slide Cart Drawer:** a cart drawer with rewards, offers and live cart tracking.
- **Web Push:** browser notifications that bring shoppers back.

You don't need to use all three. Most merchants start with one upsell offer.

## Set up your first offer in three steps

<Steps>

1. [Turn on the app embed.](/getting-started/enable-app-embed/)
2. [Create your first offer.](/getting-started/first-offer/)
3. [Check it on your store.](/getting-started/check-your-store/)

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
The app's home page has a **Setup progress** card and an **App status** section. They show which steps are done and which widgets are active.
</Callout>
