---

The app embed loads Pro Upsells on your store. **Offers can't appear until it's on**, so do this first.

## Turn it on

<Steps>

1. Open the **Pro Upsells** app and go to the Setup Guide. Or go to **Online Store > Themes** in your Shopify admin and click **Customize**.
2. Open **App embeds** in the theme editor.
3. Turn on **Pro Upsells**.
4. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  src="app-embeds.png"
  alt="The App embeds panel in the Shopify theme editor with the Pro Upsells embed switched on."
  caption="The Pro Upsells app embed, switched on."
/>

It works across your whole store, so there's no per-page setup. To turn it off later, come back to **App embeds**.

In the app, the **App status** section on the home page shows each embed as **Enabled** once it's on.

<Callout type="note">
The Slide Cart has its own app embed, **Pro Upsells: Slide Cart**. Turn it on only if you want the Slide Cart. See [Set up the Slide Cart](/slide-cart/).
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
The embed is set per theme. If you publish a different theme, turn it on again there.
</Callout>
