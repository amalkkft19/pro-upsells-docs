The **Live Cart** tab in **Slide Cart Drawer** shows active carts on your store, so you can see what shoppers are adding.

<Screenshot
  src="live-cart.png"
  alt="The Live Cart tab listing active carts on the store, with each shopper's location and cart contents."
  caption="The Live Cart tab."
/>

## Location

The shopper's country comes from Shopify. Region and city show when the shopper has a shipping address.

## Send a notification

Open a cart and choose **Send Text Notification** or **Send Discount Code**. It appears in the drawer's Notifications tab the next time that shopper opens it.

<Callout type="note">
Shopper details like name and email need the customer data agreement on the app's home page. You're responsible for using this data lawfully, in line with your store's privacy policy.
</Callout>
