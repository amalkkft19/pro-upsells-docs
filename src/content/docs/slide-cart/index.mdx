---

The Slide Cart is a drawer that opens from the side of your store, so shoppers can review their cart without leaving the page.

<Steps>

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store > Themes** and click **Customize**.
2. Open **App embeds**.
3. Turn on **Pro Upsells: Slide Cart**.
4. Click **Save**.
5. In the app, go to **Slide Cart Drawer** and follow the setup guide.

</Steps>

Open your store, add a product to the cart and check that the drawer opens.
