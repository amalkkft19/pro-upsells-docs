## Set up a goal

<Steps>

1. Open **Customize** in **Slide Cart Drawer** and go to **Cart Goals**.
2. Turn on **Enable cart goals progress bar**.
3. Choose **Total cart value** or **Product quantity** under **Tracking type**.
4. Add a goal and set its target.
5. Choose a **Reward type** from the options in the form, such as **Free Shipping**, **Order Discount**, **Percentage (%)** or **Fixed Amount**.
6. Write the **Reward label**, **Progress message** and **Completed message**.
7. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

## Tips

- Set the first goal close to your typical order, so it feels reachable.
- Use the messages to say exactly how much more is needed.
