From **Slide Cart Drawer**, open **Customize**. The page has these tabs: **Settings**, **Translations**, **Design**, **Announcements**, **Cart Goals** and **Fields**. A live preview on the right shows your changes as you make them.

## What you can control

- **Tabs:** which tabs show in the drawer. **Show cart notifications** and **Show cart offers** turn on the Notifications and Offers tabs.
- **Announcements:** rotating messages at the top of the cart, such as free shipping notes.
- **Cart Goals:** a reward progress bar. See [Cart Goals](/slide-cart/cart-goals/).
- **Discount Code Box:** lets shoppers enter a code. Turn on **Show discount code box**.
- **Terms & Condition:** a checkbox shoppers tick before checkout.
- **Empty Cart:** the message shown with nothing in the cart. You can also turn on **Show action button** and set a **Redirection URL** for where the button goes.
- **Floating Cart:** a cart button that stays on screen. Set **Show floating cart**, **Position** and **Icon type**.
- **Toaster Notification:** a short message when a product is added.
- **Translations and colours:** edit the words and colours to match your store.

Click **Save** to publish.
