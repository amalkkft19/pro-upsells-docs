**Goal:** turn one unit into two or three.

## Try

1. **Volume Discount** on products people use up or buy for several people. See [Volume Discount](/widgets/volume-discount/).
2. **Two or three tiers**, with the best value at the quantity you most want to sell.
3. **Cart page offers** as a last reminder before checkout. Choose **Cart page** under **Display page** when you create the offer.

## Measure it

Watch units per order in Shopify analytics and your margin per order. See [Read your analytics](/guides/analytics/).
