**Goal:** get shoppers to add one more item.

## Try

1. **Cross-sell on the product page.** Use an embedded [Upsell & Cross-sell](/widgets/upsell-cross-sell/) offer with an accessory for your best seller.
2. **Frequently Bought Together.** Show the full set on the product page. See [Frequently Bought Together](/widgets/frequently-bought-together/).
3. **AI Recommendations.** Show **Complementary** products where you haven't picked pairs by hand. See [AI Recommendations](/widgets/ai-recommendations/).

## Measure it

Compare average order value before and after in Shopify analytics, and offer results in the app's **Analytics**. See [Read your analytics](/guides/analytics/).
