**Goal:** give shoppers a reason to reach a higher cart total.

## Try

1. **Cart Goals** in the Slide Cart, with a target just above your typical order. See [Cart Goals](/slide-cart/cart-goals/).
2. **Automatic Free Gift** with **Show on above order / cart value**. See [Automatic Free Gift](/widgets/automatic-free-gift/).
3. An **Announcement** in the Slide Cart that says what they'll earn. See [Customize the Slide Cart](/slide-cart/customize/).

## Measure it

Check that your average order value rises by more than the reward costs you. See [Read your analytics](/guides/analytics/).
