---
title: Send a notification
description: Write and send a Web Push notification to shoppers, or promote a single offer from an offer list.
---

Send a message to shoppers who allowed notifications. [Set up Web Push](/web-push/) first if you haven't.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Web Push** and open the **Notifications** tab.
2. Click **Create Notification**.
3. Enter a **Title**, **Description** and **Redirect URL**. An **Image URL** is optional.
4. Send it.

</Steps>

You can also promote one offer: in an offer list, use **Send Notification** on that offer.

See results in the **Analytics** tab.

## Tips

- Be specific. Say what the offer is and when it ends.
- Link to the exact product or collection, not your home page.
- Send sparingly, because too many notifications lead shoppers to turn them off.
