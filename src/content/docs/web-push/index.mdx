---
title: Set up Web Push
description: Turn on Web Push to send notifications to shoppers' browsers, even after they've left your store.
---

Web Push sends notifications to shoppers' browsers, even after they've left your store. It's off until you turn it on.

## Turn it on

<Steps>

1. In the app, go to **Web Push** and open the **Settings** tab.
2. Turn on **Enable web push notifications**.
3. Save your settings.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  src="web-push-settings.png"

/>

## What shoppers see

Shoppers see a small prompt with **Allow** and **Not now**. The browser's own permission request only appears after they click **Allow**. If they choose **Not now**, the prompt stays away for a week.

<Screenshot
  src="web-push-prompt.png"
  alt="The Web Push prompt on a store, with Allow and Not now buttons."
  caption="The prompt shoppers see on your store."
/>

## iPhone shoppers

iPhones only allow web notifications from sites added to the home screen. Turn on **Show instruction modal in iPhone** to show the steps. You can edit its text and colours in **iPhone Instructions**.
