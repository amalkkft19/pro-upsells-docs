Show products that go together, so shoppers can add them in one click.

<WidgetFacts
  bestFor="Products usually bought as a set."
  showsOn="Home, product and cart pages."

## When to use it

Use it when you have items that are naturally used together, such as a camera, a case and a memory card.

<Screenshot
  src="storefront-fbt.png"
  alt="A Frequently Bought Together offer on a store's product page, showing several products with checkboxes and a combined price."
  caption="A Frequently Bought Together offer on a product page."
/>

## Create the offer

<Steps>

1. Go to **Upsells**, open **FBT Upsell** and click **Create**.
2. Enter a **Name**.
3. Choose a **Trigger condition**.
4. Choose a **Display page** and a **Display type**.
5. Choose an **Offer type**.
6. Choose the **Offer products** under **Discounts**.
7. Set the discount.
8. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  src="offer-fbt.png"

  caption="The General tab of an FBT offer, with a live preview."
/>

## Show it on your store

**Embedded** offers need the **FBT Upsell** block on the page. See [Add a widget block to your theme](/guides/add-block-to-theme/).

<Callout type="note">
On the home page with a trigger for specific products or collections, **Popup** is the only display type. The home page has no product in context, so the offer can only open when a shopper adds one of those products to the cart.
</Callout>

## Tips

- Use products that really do sell together. Your order history is the best source.
- A small discount on the set gives shoppers a reason to add everything.

## If it isn't working

- The **FBT Upsell** block isn't on the page.
- The trigger doesn't match the product being viewed.
- **Hide out-of-stock products** is on and the offer's products are sold out.

For the full checklist, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/).
