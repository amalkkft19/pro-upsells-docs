Show recommended products automatically, without choosing them one by one. The recommendations come from Shopify's product recommendations.

<WidgetFacts
  bestFor="Large catalogues, or when you don't want to pick products by hand."
  showsOn="Home, product, collection and cart pages."
  needsBlock="Only for **Embedded** offers."
/>

## When to use it

Use it when your catalogue is too large to pair products by hand, or you'd rather not pick them one by one.

<Screenshot
  src="storefront-ai.png"
  alt="An AI Recommendations offer on a store's product page, showing recommended products with prices and Add buttons."
  caption="An AI Recommendations offer on a product page."
/>

## Create the offer

Choose a **Recommendation type**:

- **Related:** products similar to the one being viewed.
- **Complementary:** products that go well with it.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Upsells**, open **AI Recommendations** and click **Create**.
2. Enter a **Name**.
3. Choose a **Trigger condition**.
4. Choose a **Display page** and a **Display type**.
5. Choose a **Recommendation type** and a **Limit** under **Product Recommendation**.
6. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  src="offer-ai-recommendations.png"
  alt="The General tab of an AI Recommendations offer. It shows the trigger condition, the display page options and the Product Recommendation section with Related selected, plus a live preview."
  caption="The General tab of an AI Recommendations offer, with a live preview."
/>

## Show it on your store

**Embedded** offers need the **AI Recommendation** block. See [Add a widget block to your theme](/guides/add-block-to-theme/).

<Callout type="note">
On the home page, set the trigger to specific products and add the products to recommend from. The home page has no product of its own to base recommendations on.
</Callout>

## Tips

- Start with **Related** on product pages.
- New stores with little sales history may see fewer recommendations.

## If it isn't working

- The **AI Recommendation** block isn't on the page.
- Shopify has no recommendations for that product yet.
- On the home page, no specific products are set.

For the full checklist, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/).
