Offer a better price when shoppers buy more of the same product.

<WidgetFacts
  bestFor="Consumables, gifts and products people buy in multiples."
  showsOn="Product, cart and checkout (Shopify Plus only) pages."
  needsBlock="Yes, for **Embedded** offers on product and cart pages. Checkout uses a checkout block."
/>

## When to use it

Use it when you can afford a lower margin per unit in exchange for more units per order.

<Screenshot
  src="storefront-volume.png"
  alt="A Volume Discount offer on a store's product page, showing quantity tiers and the saving for each."
  caption="A Volume Discount offer on a product page."
/>

## Create the offer

<Steps>

1. Go to **Upsells**, open **Volume Discount** and click **Create**.
2. Enter a **Name**.
3. Choose a **Trigger condition**.
4. Choose a **Display page** and a **Display type**.
5. Set **Discount #1** under **Discounts**: the **Discount type**, **Discount value** and **Minimum purchase quantity**.
6. Click **Add More Offers** to add more tiers, such as buy 2 and buy 3.
7. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  src="offer-volume-discount.png"

## Show it on your store

**Embedded** offers on product and cart pages need the **Volume Discount** block. See [Add a widget block to your theme](/guides/add-block-to-theme/).

<Callout type="plus">
The **Checkout page** option needs Shopify Plus. **Embedded** is the only display type there, and the offer shows only after you add the **Volume Discount** block in the checkout editor. See [Add a block to checkout, thank you or order status](/guides/checkout-thank-you-blocks/).
</Callout>

## Tips

- Two or three tiers are easier to compare than many.
- Make each tier's saving clear, and make the best deal worth reaching.

## If it isn't working

- The **Volume Discount** block isn't on the page.
- The trigger doesn't match the product being viewed.

For the full checklist, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/).
