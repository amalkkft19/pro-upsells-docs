## When to use it

Use it when you know which product pairs well with another, or you have a better version of a popular product.

## Create the offer

Choose the **Offer type** that matches what you want to sell:

- **Upsell:** replaces the trigger product with the upsell product. Use it to move a shopper up to a better product.
- **Cross-sell:** adds a product alongside the trigger product. Use it for accessories and add-ons.
- **None:** the discount doesn't need a trigger product. Use it to offer a discounted product to anyone.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Upsells**, open **Upsells & Cross-sell** and click **Create**.
2. Enter a **Name**.
3. Choose a **Trigger condition** under **When should the upsell be shown?**. The choices are **Show for all products**, **Show for specific products**, **Show for specific collections** and **Show on above order / cart value**.
4. Choose a **Display page** and a **Display type**.
5. Choose an **Offer type**.
6. Choose the **Offer products** under **Discounts**.
7. Set the **Discount type**, **Discount value** and **Minimum purchase quantity**.
8. Optional: click **Add More Offers** to add more offers.
9. Fine-tune the offer in the **Settings**, **Translations** and **Design** tabs. See [Offer settings explained](/guides/offer-settings/).
10. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

## Show it on your store

**Embedded** offers need the **Upsell & Cross-sell** block. Offers on the checkout, thank you and order status pages use a checkout block instead. See [Add a widget block to your theme](/guides/add-block-to-theme/) and [Add a block to checkout, thank you or order status](/guides/checkout-thank-you-blocks/).

## Tips

- Recommend products that make sense with the trigger, at a lower price than the main item.
- Keep to a few products per offer, so the choice stays easy.
- Turn on **Hide product if already in cart** so shoppers aren't offered what they already have.

## If it isn't working

- The app embed is off.
- The offer is **Embedded** with no block on that page.
- The trigger doesn't match the product being viewed.

For the full checklist, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/).
