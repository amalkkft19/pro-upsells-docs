Add a free product to the cart automatically when it matches your conditions.

## When to use it

Use it to reward shoppers who reach a cart value you choose, or to put a new product in front of people who are already buying.

## Create the offer

<Steps>

1. Go to **Upsells**, open **Automatic Free Gift** and click **Create**.
2. Enter a **Name**.
3. Choose when the gift applies under **Trigger condition**. For example, **Show on above order / cart value** rewards carts above an amount you set.
4. Choose the gift product under **Discounts**.
5. To make it free, set **Discount type** to **Percentage** and **Discount value** to 100.
6. Optional: click **Add More Offers** to add more gifts.
7. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

## Show it on your store

There is nothing to place on your store. The offer runs in the background, and the gift is added directly to the cart when the cart matches your trigger.

<Callout type="note">
The app embed must be on, and the cart must already have a product in it. A shopper can't get the gift on an empty cart.
</Callout>

## Tips

- Set the cart value just above your typical order, so reaching it takes one more item.
- Choose a gift with a low cost to you and a clear value to shoppers.
- Mention the gift somewhere visible, such as a Slide Cart announcement, because the gift has no widget of its own.
- Make sure the gift product has stock. If it's sold out, it isn't added and shoppers aren't told.

## If it isn't working

- The app embed is off.
- The gift product is sold out.
- The cart doesn't meet the trigger.

For the full checklist, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/).
